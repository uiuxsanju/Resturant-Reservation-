Our Story

Founded in 2025, Catch of the Day as a small family-owned bistro with a passion for bringing people together through exceptional food. Today, we continue that tradition with a commitment to quality and innovation.

Our chef brings 20 years of international experience, blending traditional techniques with modern creativity to deliver unforgettable dining experiences.
